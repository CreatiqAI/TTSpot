## Full description (max 4000)
TT Spot is the map for Malaysia's car community. See where the meets are, who's out tonight and which spots are worth the drive. Your car is your profile.

ONE LIVE MAP
• Car meets, TT sessions, convoys and track days near you
• Car cafés and good spots, with the ones you save always on your map
• Start a "TT now" and let your friends know where you are
• One tap to the meet in Waze or Google Maps, and "I'm on my way" posts your ETA to the meet chat

YOUR CAR, YOUR PROFILE
• Snap one photo and TT Spot works out the make, model, year and specs
• A garage for all your cars: pick today's car and it goes with you to meets
• Log your mods and what you spent

SHOW UP, EARN, COLLECT
• Check in at meets and spots with a QR scan or GPS and earn points
• Your first blind box is a welcome gift: shake your phone to open it
• Collect all seven TiTi cards, trade doubles with friends, swap cards for prizes
• Claim vouchers from partner car shops

FOR ORGANIZERS
• Verified organizers get crew roles, scheduled announcements and a free lucky draw with a live stage screen
• Floorplans with levels for car parks, malls and expo halls
• A turnout report with verified check-ins after every meet
• An invite QR so new people can join your event in the app

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• You choose who sees you on the map: friends, nearby, everyone or nobody
• Ghost mode hides you any time
• Report or block anyone; reports are reviewed within 24 hours

TT Spot is for drivers aged 18 and over. Drive safe, respect the roads and keep meets legal. Lucky draws are free to enter; prizes are provided by the event organizer.
