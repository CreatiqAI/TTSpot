## Full description (max 4000)
TT Spot is the map for Malaysia's car community. See where the meets are, who's out tonight and which spots are worth the drive.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Find car meets, TT sessions and convoys on a live map
• Start a "TT now" and let your friends know where you are
• Check in at meets and spots with a QR scan or GPS
• Show off your garage: your cars, photos and mods
• Chat with friends, clubs and everyone at a meet
• Join car clubs and see your clubmates on the map
• Post photos, moments and polls
• Earn points for showing up and claim vouchers from partner car shops

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• You choose who sees your car on the map: friends, nearby, everyone or nobody
• Ghost mode hides you any time
• Location is only used while the app is open
• Report or block anyone; reports are reviewed within 24 hours

FOR CLUBS, HOSTS AND SHOPS
• Clubs get their own page, member roles and club meets
• Hosts get a turnout report with verified check-ins after every meet
• Partner shops get a shop page, vouchers and scan-to-redeem

TT Spot is for drivers aged 18 and over. Drive safe, respect the roads and keep meets legal.
