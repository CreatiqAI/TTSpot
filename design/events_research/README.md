# Malaysian car events, 9 Oct 2026 to end of Jan 2027

Research done 8-9 Oct 2026. `events.json` holds 12 events, sorted by start date. Each date was checked against at least one official or reputable source. Coordinates come from OpenStreetMap Nominatim.

The best single source was Sepang International Circuit's own calendar feed (`https://www.sepangcircuit.com/eventcalendar/index/getevents`, the data behind `/event-calendar`). It lists every booking at the circuit up to 31 Dec 2026.

Notes:
- **Open Track Days**: you drive your own car (RM400 per slot); they are not spectator events. The dates match the SIC event page, the SIC shop and the calendar feed. If five of them make the tab too repetitive, keep one or two.
- **MSF Round 4**: the MSF site gives 17-18 Oct. The SIC feed shows the 17th as "private booking" and the 18th as "MSF Racing", so the Saturday may be qualifying or closed to the public.
- **Sepang 1000KM** is medium confidence. The dates are only an event-week block in the SIC feed (18-22 Nov) plus a "2026" ticket tile on the SIC ticketing page. The race-day timetable and 2026 prices aren't published yet, so the start and end times are placeholders.
- **Sepang 12 Hours**: the dates appear in the SIC feed and in the organiser's April 2026 announcement (race24.asia). Spectator ticket details aren't out yet.
- **Hot Wheels Monster Trucks** is a ticketed arena show, not a car meet. It's included because it's the biggest car-themed public event in the window. Most sessions were nearly sold out on Tri-Tix when checked.
- **MIAPEX**: dates and venue come from Bernama (15 Sep 2026). The hours (10am-6pm) come from TheSmartLocal. The official site, miapex.com, returned an empty page, so the Details link points to Bernama. Admission price wasn't confirmed.
- **Times**: SIC events use the circuit's own 09:00 start. Other events use published opening times, and 10:00 where none was published.

Unconfirmed or wrong dates:

Out of scope (not car events):

- **EVM Asia** (10-12 Nov, MITEC) is a trade-only manufacturing expo.

## Coverage gap

Every confirmed event is in the Klang Valley or at Sepang. Searches for Penang, Johor, Perak, Melaka, Sabah and Sarawak in Oct 2026-Jan 2027 found no announced public car events. Club meets and mall auto expos are usually announced only 2-4 weeks ahead on Facebook and Instagram, so it's worth checking again in early November and early December.
